Any pump.fun coin whose on-chain creator you are can have its fees redirected. The fees stop going to your wallet and start going to the coin's PURPS wallet, which runs the strategy you choose.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the coin">
    `GET /redirects/{mint}` returns `feeWallet` (where the fees must go), `redirected` (whether they already do) and `registered` (whether a strategy is attached).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route the fees">
    If not redirected: `POST /pumpfun/fees` with `mint` and `creatorWallet`, sign the returned transaction with the creator wallet, and send it through `/transactions/send`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register the strategy">
    `POST /redirects` with `mint` and the strategy as `config`: a preset and the markets, `{ "preset": "balanced", "markets": ["ETH"] }`, with every option on the [purps.lol page](/guides/launch-solana#a-strategy-launch). The full config works too. No signature needed: the fee routing on chain is the proof. If it answers `registered: false`, the chain hasn't shown us the routing yet. Wait a few seconds and call it again. `registered: true` means the coin is live on purps.lol with its strategy running.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A coin's strategy is set once, at registration. A coin launched through `POST /pumpfun/launches` with a `strategy` in the build doesn't come here: we register that one ourselves once its fee routing lands.
