---
title: "Manage a coin you launched"
description: "Creator-signed changes to a launchpad coin."
---

Both changes are signed by the launch's creator wallet, the same prepare-sign-submit way.

## Attach a strategy later

A launchpad coin launched with the `airdrop` or `split` preset can have a strategy attached afterwards. `POST /coins/{mint}/strategy/prepare` with `config`, sign, then `POST /coins/{mint}/strategy/submit` with the identical `config`, `ts` and `signature`.

A `creator`-preset launch cannot: its fee vault is owned by your wallet on chain and the strategy could never claim from it.

## Change what holders are paid in

For a coin that pays its holders: `POST /coins/{mint}/payout/prepare` with `payoutChoice`, sign, then `POST /coins/{mint}/payout/submit`.

| `payoutChoice` | Holders receive |
| --- | --- |
| `coin` | The coin itself. |
| `auto` | The asset backing the strategy's largest perp leg. |
| a mint address | That backed asset. |

A burn coin is refused; switching from burning to paying is an admin decision.
