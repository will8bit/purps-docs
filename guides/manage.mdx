Prepare, sign, submit. The signature must come from the launch's `creatorWallet` (shown on `GET /launches/{mint}`); prepare takes only the `config`.

## Attach a strategy later

A purps.lol coin launched without a strategy can get one afterwards. From then on the whole creator fee goes into it. `POST /coins/{mint}/strategy/prepare` with `config`, sign, then `POST /coins/{mint}/strategy/submit` with the same `config`, `ts` and `signature`. The `config` is a preset and the markets, `{ "preset": "balanced", "markets": ["ETH"] }`, with every option on the [purps.lol page](/guides/launch-solana#a-strategy-launch). The full config works too.

Prepare validates the `config` first: a malformed one is a `400` that names the field, so you don't sign anything for nothing. The message carries the strategy in words on a `sets:` line (the fee split, what profits do, the take-profit and dip-buy rules, the markets) next to its hash. A short form is expanded before the words and the hash are made, so the message describes the full strategy it becomes, and sending the same short form to submit gives the same hash.

Launches from before September 2026 can't do this: their fee vault is owned by the creator's wallet on chain and the strategy could never claim from it.

## Changing a strategy that is already running

That's [creator authority](/guides/creator-authority): the owner signs each change, and it's published on the coin's Changes tab.
