## Attach a strategy later

A purps.lol coin launched without a strategy can have one attached afterwards; from then on the whole creator fee goes into it. `POST /coins/{mint}/strategy/prepare` with `config`, sign, then `POST /coins/{mint}/strategy/submit` with the identical `config`, `ts` and `signature`. The `config` is the strategy object from the Solana launch guide, `payoutMint` and up to four `payoutMintsExtra` included.

Prepare validates the `config` first: a malformed one is a `400` that names the field, so nothing is signed for nothing. The message it returns carries the strategy in words on a `sets:` line (the fee split, what profits do, the take-profit and dip-buy rules, the markets) next to its hash; read it before signing.

Launches from before September 2026 cannot: their fee vault is owned by the creator's wallet on chain and the strategy could never claim from it.

## Changing a strategy that is already running

That is [creator authority](/guides/creator-authority): the owner signs each change, and it is published on the coin's Changes tab.
